What is this?
This I learned that changed. A list of things I once knew that I should no longer know. I'll try to include evidence where I can. Some of these things might even just be personal misconceptions. I don't mean for this to have any real agenda, so I will focus on scientific facts (or facts that were presented (to me) with the authority or claims of science). Some of the facts (before the updates from science) are actually more like assumptions or implicit understandings.
I'll categorize the facts based on subject, but there are two types of things here: facts that I learned that I needed to unlearn and events that led to something but that I hadn't followed up on. Also this isn't meant to criticize: I think this is an inevitable consequence of having a good education where you'd learn some fairly current stuff and not get an update and also just how information works: it's hard to expect change so you don't get updates unless you're either proactive or lucky to just hear something.
